Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

This policy covers mkk-palilula.com. In short: the site has no accounts, forms or newsletter, and it uses Google Analytics only if you accept it.

Who is responsible

mkk-palilula.com is run by MKK Palilula. Until there's a contact email, you can reach us on X (@mkk_palilula) or GitHub (MKK-Palilula).

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you open a page, GitHub receives the request, including your IP address, and may keep it in its logs for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Google Analytics, with your consent

If you accept in the cookie banner, the site loads Google Analytics, a service of Google Ireland Limited, to count visits and see which pages are read. It sets cookies (_ga and _ga_…, kept for up to two years) and sends Google the pages you visit, where you came from, your browser and device type, and your approximate location, worked out from your IP address. Google may process this data in the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. See how Google uses this data.

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we take that as a no: the banner doesn't appear and Google Analytics doesn't load, unless you accept it yourself in Cookie settings.

The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). If you decline, nothing from Google loads and no analytics cookie is set. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page; withdrawing consent deletes the analytics cookies.

Your choice, in your browser

Your answer to the cookie banner is kept in your browser's local storage, so the banner doesn't ask again. It never leaves your device.

Links to other sites

The site links to profiles on GitHub, X, Instagram, YouTube and itch.io, and to other sites. Nothing from them loads until you follow a link; once you do, their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data held about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw your consent at any time. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
